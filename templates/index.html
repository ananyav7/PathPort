How PathPort Works in 3 Simple Steps

Fast, secure, and intuitive for both senders and commuters

1

1. Post Your Parcel

Enter origin, destination, parcel photo, and size. Our system calculates optimal rewards and carbon offset instantly.

2

2. Match with Commuter

Commuters already traveling along that route review the request and accept. Verified contact info and route details are shared.

3. Dual OTP Handover
